03_BUILD_METHODOLOGY/RRM_PLAYBOOK.md:87: **Branch flow (Director ruling RUL-02):** the module has a numbered engineering branch (for example `rrm-001-<app>`). After Engineering, the Director commits and pushes the completed engineering work and creates the matching `qa/<module-branch>` **at that same commit** — the completed engineering candidate. The Engineer performs authorized rework on that QA branch; the QA Executor independently tests on that same branch; role boundaries are preserved and conflicting simultaneous writes are avoided (one active writer when sessions share a worktree). The QA Lead certifies the governed candidate; the Architect issues the closeout instruction; the Engineer performs bounded closeout; the Director commits, merges the QA branch into `main` and pushes `main`. **Repairs are not transferred back to the engineering branch and the QA branch is not abandoned as the normal workflow.** Product, execution, evidence and closeout identities are preserved separately.

03_BUILD_METHODOLOGY/RRM_PLAYBOOK.md:93: Every RRM includes QAM: the Architect instantiates `QAM/` from the Hub template set at packaging; the Engineer completes the factual manifest and engineering handoff as part of the engineering assignment; the QA Executor performs Q1 (independent recon and test plan) and, after QA Lead approval and Director release, Q2 (continuous execution); the QA Lead adjudicates findings and certifies (`QAM_PLAYBOOK.md` §§5–14). 03_BUILD_METHODOLOGY/RRM_PLAYBOOK.md:105: A repair instruction names the affected findings/ACs, allowed files, prohibited changes and required engineering checks.
