Copy scripts/ and RAW/context.json there, updating only evidence/scratch filesystem locations in the copied context. The review repo, execution worktree and pinned commit identities remain the named inputs. Run 01_preflight.py, 02_measure.py, 03_manual_evidence.py, 04_end.py with `python3 -B -I <script>` in that order. Network access is needed only for the two read-only ls-remote pin checks. Do not bypass a pin mismatch. Scripts 01/02/04 derive mechanical evidence; 03 records this Executor's explicit semantic decisions and must be independently read, not treated as an independent oracle.
