# Delivery layout and provenance

RUN/ is the complete execution-run record with additive closeout post-commit receipts overlaid from the review repository. These later receipts (COMMIT_COMMANDS, COMMIT_RECEIPT, PUSH_COMMANDS, POST_COMMIT_RECEIPT, FINAL_VERIFICATION, FABLE_HANDOFF and this note) are outside their own commit to preserve the clean execution tree. PUBLICATION_SCOPE.json enumerates precommit payload excluding itself; COMMIT_RECEIPT.json enumerates all43 committed paths. No frozen file is changed by the overlay. INSTALLED_SUCCESSOR_CONTENT/ contains all101 installed successor files plus lint infrastructure and standalone10x for inspection. The prior accepted package remains in Downloads with its recorded hash. PACKAGE_SHA256SUMS.txt covers every other ZIP member; external ZIP SHA is supplied in the delivery response.
